Alexandros Graikos

I am a PhD candidate at Stony Brook University, advised by Dimitris Samaras. My research revolves around the image priors encoded in deep generative denoising models, and their applications to the digital histopathology and satellite image domains.

I also interned at Microsoft Research where with my mentor Nebojsa Jojic we worked on generative prior problems.

Alexandros Graikos, Nebojsa Jojic, Dimitris Samaras

Preprint

In denoisers, the eigenvectors of the Jacobian encode generation quality. Increasing the spectrum during training improves generation quality.
